Companies,
built from zero.

Reports point to OFF/BEAT working as a venture studio: starting new companies in-house and taking a stake in them. This page imagines how that could feel from a founder's side.

How a build could run.

  1. Start with a problem

    Ideas come from the studio's own work on creators, audiences and what AI now makes possible, or from founders who arrive with one.

  2. Form the first team

    A small founding group gathers around the idea, with design, engineering and content help from inside the studio.

  3. Test with real people

    A first version reaches a small audience through creators, before anything is scaled up.

  4. Scale what works

    Companies that hold up grow with the studio's backing, with OFF/BEAT holding a stake.
