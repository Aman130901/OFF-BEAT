Failed once?
Bring it back.

Aman Gupta put up a billboard outside The Camellias in Gurugram inviting failed founders to build with OFF/BEAT, then another at Antilia. This page picks up that invitation.

What to bring.

  • /The idea that stayed

    The one you kept thinking about after the last company shut down.

  • /What it taught you

    What went wrong, and what you would do differently with the same problem.

  • /Why it works now

    What has changed in the market, in the technology, or in you.

This checklist is written for the concept. OFF/BEAT has not published a founder application process.
